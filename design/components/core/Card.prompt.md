The "game cartridge" card — GameShelf's one card motif, reused for the player card, auth form, and any future content block. Thick ink border, offset drop-shadow, gold title bar.

```jsx
<Card ridges>
  <CardTitleBar>Player Card</CardTitleBar>
  <CardBody>...</CardBody>
</Card>
```

`ridges` adds the teal cartridge-top notch bar (used on the home player card, not the auth card). `CardTitleBar` is the bold gold header strip; `CardBody` is the padded content grid.
